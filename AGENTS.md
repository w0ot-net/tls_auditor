# AGENTS.md

Guidance for AI agents working in this repository.

## Workflow rules

- **Always commit and push after every change.** Once a change is complete and
  verified, stage the relevant files, commit with a clear message, and push to
  `origin/main`. Do not leave finished work sitting uncommitted in the working
  tree.
  - Do **not** commit generated artifacts such as `__pycache__/`.
